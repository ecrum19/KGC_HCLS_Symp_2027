# Additional Resources

## VCF Core

An ontology for representing Variant Call Format (VCF) data as RDF.

Submitted as a research paper to the [SWAT4HCLS 2027 conference](https://www.swat4ls.org/) (Basel, 1–4 February 2027).

- **Submission (long paper, revised):** [main_long.pdf](https://github.com/ecrum19/SWAT4HCLS_2027/blob/main/long_revised/main_long.pdf)
- **Ontology:** <https://w3id.org/vcf-core#>

## VCF-RDFizer

A tool for converting VCF files into RDF knowledge graphs.

Currently in peer review at the [Journal of Biomedical Semantics](https://link.springer.com/journal/13326), for its SWAT4HCLS special collection, [*Semantic Technologies in Healthcare and Life Sciences*](https://link.springer.com/collections/fhajdigahi).

- **Submission:** [submitted.pdf](https://github.com/ecrum19/vcf-rdfizer-testing/blob/main/BioMedSem_2026/paper/submitted.pdf)
- **Source code:** <https://github.com/ecrum19/VCF-RDFizer>
